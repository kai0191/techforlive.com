<div class="key"><b>In short:</b> an NPU (neural processing unit) is a part of the chip designed to run AI tasks very efficiently, such as background blur in video calls, live captions, photo search and on-device assistants. It saves battery compared with doing the same work on the CPU or GPU. Most laptop processors launched since 2024 include one.</div>

<h2>CPU vs GPU vs NPU</h2>
<table><thead><tr><th>Part</th><th>Good at</th><th>Example jobs</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td>CPU</td><td>General tasks, quick decisions, running the system</td><td>Apps, browsing, spreadsheets</td></tr>
<tr><td>GPU</td><td>Massive parallel maths, very high performance</td><td>Games, video editing, training and running large AI models</td></tr>
<tr><td>NPU</td><td>AI maths at low power, for hours</td><td>Camera effects, noise removal, live translation, local assistants</td></tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>A GPU can often run AI faster than an NPU, but it uses much more power. The NPU's job is efficiency: running AI features constantly without draining the battery.</p>

<h2>What does TOPS mean?</h2>
<p><b>TOPS</b> means trillions of operations per second, a rough measure of AI throughput, usually quoted for low-precision maths. Microsoft requires an NPU of at least 40 TOPS for a laptop to be sold as a <b>Copilot+ PC</b>. Like megapixels on cameras, TOPS is a guide rather than the whole story: software support matters more.</p>

<h2>Which chips have NPUs?</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>Intel Core Ultra</b> processors (see our <a href="/intel-computer-processor-i3-i5-i7-which-best-for-you/">Intel naming guide</a>).</li>
<li><b>AMD Ryzen AI</b> laptop processors.</li>
<li><b>Qualcomm Snapdragon X</b> series in Windows laptops.</li>
<li><b>Apple silicon</b> Macs, iPhones and iPads (Apple calls it the Neural Engine).</li>
<li>Recent flagship phone chips from Google, Samsung, Qualcomm and MediaTek.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Features that use the NPU today</h2>
<ul>
<li>Windows Studio Effects: background blur, eye contact and voice focus in calls.</li>
<li>Live Captions with translation on Copilot+ PCs.</li>
<li>Natural-language search for files and settings on Copilot+ PCs.</li>
<li>Apple Intelligence features on supported Macs, iPhones and iPads.</li>
<li>Photo and video apps that use AI for selections, upscaling and noise reduction.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Should you pay extra for an "AI PC"?</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>Yes, if</b> you buy a new laptop anyway: most mid-range and better models now include an NPU, and the efficiency gains help battery life on video calls.</li>
<li><b>Do not upgrade a good laptop</b> just for the NPU; the features are nice extras, not essentials.</li>
<li><b>For serious local AI</b> (running large language models or image generators), RAM and a strong GPU matter far more than the NPU.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Quick answers</h2>
<h3>Does an NPU make games faster?</h3>
<p>No. Games use the CPU and GPU. Some upscaling technologies use AI, but they run on the graphics card.</p>
<h3>Do I need an NPU to use ChatGPT or Copilot?</h3>
<p>No. Those chatbots run in the cloud. The NPU matters for features that run locally on your device.</p>
