<div class="key"><b>In short:</b> free cloud plans are great for documents and phone photos, but most are <em>sync</em> services, not true backups: if you delete or a virus encrypts a file, the change syncs everywhere. Combine one free cloud service with an external drive and you have a backup that survives theft, fire and ransomware.</div>

<h2>Sync is not backup</h2>
<p>Sync keeps the same files on all your devices. That is convenient, but it copies mistakes too: delete a folder on your laptop and it vanishes from the cloud and your phone. A real backup keeps <b>older versions</b> you can roll back to. Many sync services keep deleted files for 30 days or offer version history, which helps, but a separate copy is safer.</p>

<h2>The 3-2-1 rule, made simple</h2>
<ol class="steps">
<li><b>3 copies</b> of anything you cannot replace: the original plus two backups.</li>
<li><b>2 different types of storage</b>, for example your laptop and an external drive.</li>
<li><b>1 copy off-site</b>: a cloud service, or a drive kept at a relative's house.</li>
</ol>
<p>A practical version for most homes: phone photos to Google Photos or iCloud, computer documents to OneDrive or Drive, and a monthly full copy to an external drive that you unplug afterwards (so ransomware cannot reach it).</p>

<h2>Built-in tools you already have</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>Windows 11:</b> Windows Backup (OneDrive folders and settings) and File History for versioned backups to an external drive.</li>
<li><b>macOS:</b> Time Machine to an external drive keeps hourly, daily and weekly snapshots automatically.</li>
<li><b>Android:</b> Settings › Google › Backup; Google Photos for pictures.</li>
<li><b>iPhone:</b> iCloud Backup, or a local backup to a Mac or PC.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Quick answers</h2>
<h3>Which free service gives the most space?</h3>
<p>MEGA currently offers the largest permanent free tier of the big names, at 20 GB. Google's 15 GB is the most useful for Android and Gmail users.</p>
<h3>Are free cloud backups private?</h3>
<p>Your files are encrypted in transit and on the provider's servers, but most providers hold the keys. For sensitive documents, choose an end-to-end encrypted service or encrypt files before uploading.</p>
<h3>How often should I back up?</h3>
<p>Continuously for phone photos and documents (let the app do it), and at least monthly for a full external-drive copy.</p>
