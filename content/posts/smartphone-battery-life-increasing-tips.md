<div class="key"><b>The three that matter most:</b> lower screen brightness (or use auto-brightness), find and restrict the app that is draining in the background (check the battery usage screen), and keep your phone out of the heat. For long-term battery health, turn on your phone's charge-limit or optimised charging option.</div>

<h2>Find the real drain first</h2>
<p>Open <b>Settings › Battery</b> on both iPhone and Android. The usage list shows which apps used the most power in the last 24 hours and 10 days. If one app you barely opened sits near the top, it is working in the background: restrict it, update it, or delete it.</p>

<h2>Daily tips that make a real difference</h2>
<ol class="steps">
<li><b>Use auto-brightness and a shorter screen timeout.</b> The display is usually the single biggest power user.</li>
<li><b>Use dark mode on OLED screens.</b> Black pixels on OLED panels are switched off, so dark themes save energy; on LCD screens the saving is negligible.</li>
<li><b>Lower the refresh rate if needed.</b> 120 Hz looks smooth but costs battery. Most phones switch adaptively; forcing 60 Hz helps on older models.</li>
<li><b>Restrict background activity</b> for apps that do not need it (Android: app › Battery › Restricted; iPhone: Settings › General › Background App Refresh).</li>
<li><b>Prune location access.</b> Set apps to "While using" rather than "Always".</li>
<li><b>Cut notifications</b> you never read. Each one wakes the screen and radio.</li>
<li><b>Prefer Wi-Fi over mobile data</b> when available; a weak mobile signal makes the radio work harder.</li>
<li><b>Use Low Power Mode / Battery Saver</b> when you know the day will be long. It limits background work and visual effects.</li>
<li><b>Remove widgets that refresh constantly</b> (live news, stocks) if you do not look at them.</li>
<li><b>Update your phone and apps.</b> Updates often fix battery-draining bugs.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Myths you can ignore</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>"Close all your apps."</b> Force-closing apps usually costs more battery, because the phone has to reload them from scratch. Only close an app that is misbehaving.</li>
<li><b>"Drain it to 0% every time."</b> That was for old nickel batteries. Lithium-ion cells prefer shallow cycles; deep discharges age them faster.</li>
<li><b>"Turning off Bluetooth saves a lot."</b> Modern Bluetooth Low Energy uses very little power. Turn it off if you like, but expect a small gain.</li>
<li><b>"Overnight charging ruins batteries."</b> Phones stop charging when full, and optimised charging delays the last 20% until just before you wake.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Keep the battery healthy for years</h2>
<p>Lithium-ion batteries wear out faster when they are hot or sit at 100% for long periods. To slow that:</p>
<ul>
<li>Turn on <b>Optimised Battery Charging</b> or the <b>80% limit</b> on iPhone, or <b>Adaptive Charging / Battery Protection</b> on Android (names vary by brand).</li>
<li>Avoid gaming or fast charging while the phone is hot, and do not leave it in a car or in direct sun.</li>
<li>If storing a phone for months, leave it around half charged.</li>
</ul>
<p>Check health under <b>Settings › Battery › Battery Health</b> on iPhone, or the battery or device care section on many Android phones. Most manufacturers consider a battery worn when it holds about 80% of its original capacity.</p>

<h2>When tips are not enough</h2>
<p>If health is below about 80% or the phone shuts down unexpectedly, a battery replacement is far cheaper than a new phone. For long days, a power bank is the simplest fix.</p>

<h2>Quick answers</h2>
<h3>Does fast charging damage my battery?</h3>
<p>Phones manage fast charging carefully and slow down as the battery fills. Heat is the bigger enemy, so avoid fast charging a phone that is already hot.</p>
<h3>Is it bad to use my phone while charging?</h3>
<p>Light use is fine. Heavy gaming while fast charging creates a lot of heat, which ages the battery faster.</p>
<h3>Why did my battery life drop after an update?</h3>
<p>For a day or two after a major update the phone re-indexes files and photos in the background. If it does not recover after a few days, check the usage list for a misbehaving app.</p>
