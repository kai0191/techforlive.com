Ohm's law in one line

The resistor drops whatever voltage the LEDs do not use: R = (Vsupply − Vled × count) ÷ I. With 5 V, a red LED (2 V) and 15 mA, that is 3 V ÷ 0.015 A = 200 Ω. Standard resistors come in fixed steps, so we round up to the next E12 value, which keeps the current slightly lower and safer.

Power in the resistor is V × I. Ordinary 1/4 W (0.25 W) through-hole resistors are fine for single LEDs; if the result gets close to 0.25 W, use a 1/2 W part.

Raspberry Pi GPIO pins are 3.3 V and not designed for heavy loads; keep LED current modest there and drive anything bigger through a transistor.
