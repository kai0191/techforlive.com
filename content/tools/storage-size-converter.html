The 7% that "goes missing"

Storage makers define 1 TB as 1,000,000,000,000 bytes. Windows divides by 1024 at every step (it really shows tebibytes and gibibytes, but labels them TB and GB). One trillion bytes ÷ 1024⁴ = 0.909, so a "1 TB" drive appears as about 931 GB. Nothing is missing; the two numbers just use different rulers.

macOS has used decimal units since 2009, so a Mac shows the same number as the box. Formatting and the file system also reserve a little space, and phones subtract the operating system before you get any.

The "what fits" figures are rough averages. Photo sizes depend heavily on camera resolution and format (HEIC is smaller than JPEG), and video depends on bitrate.
