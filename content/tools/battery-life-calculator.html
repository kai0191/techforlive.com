How the maths works

mAh alone does not tell you how much energy a battery holds, because it ignores voltage. Energy in watt-hours is mAh × V ÷ 1000. A 10,000 mAh power bank at 3.7 V stores 37 Wh.

When a power bank charges a phone it boosts its cell voltage to 5 V or more for the USB cable, and the phone converts it back down. Each step turns some energy into heat, so only part of the 37 Wh reaches your phone. That is why a 10,000 mAh bank does not give two full charges to a 5,000 mAh phone.

Runtime is simply usable watt-hours divided by the device's draw in watts. Real devices vary their draw constantly, so treat the result as a planning estimate.
